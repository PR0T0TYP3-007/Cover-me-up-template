Privacy notice

Plain words, short version. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your HMO, plan type and hospital name, so we can apply the right rules.
  • The message you type or say, and the answer we give you.
  • Your name and phone number, only when you write a complaint.
  • We never ask for your diagnosis or medical records.

Who reads your message

An AI service (Google's Gemini) reads your message to understand it and to write your script. Whether something is covered is decided by our published rules, not by the AI. Voice notes are processed in memory and never stored.

How we protect it

  • No account is needed to get help.
  • Names, phone numbers and free text are encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Our logs never contain your message, name or phone number.

How long we keep it

Closed cases are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Your rights

You can delete everything, any time: Settings → Delete all my data. This removes your details, cases, timelines and complaints in one step.

Not medical advice

CoverMe gives information about your insurance rights. It does not give medical advice. In an emergency, get treatment first.
